# Josephine – how to run your shop from your iPhone

## First time
1. Open **your-shop-address/admin** in Safari and log in with your password.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. From now on open the admin from the home screen icon.
3. In the admin tap **More → Turn on notifications**. You will get a message on your phone for every new order.
4. In **More** check your MoMo number, WhatsApp number and add your Instagram and TikTok links.

## Add a product
1. **Products → + Add**.
2. Name, category, price (just the number, e.g. `45000`). Old price only if it is on sale.
3. Colours and sizes: type one and tap **Add** (for heels, clothes and wigs there is a button for the usual sizes).
4. Type how many pieces you have of each colour/size. When a product reaches 0, it disappears from the shop by itself.
5. Tap **Create product**, then **📷 Add photos** – take photos in daylight on a plain background. The first photo is the main one (tap **★ Main** to change it).
6. Tick **Josephine's pick** to show it on the home page.

## When an order comes in
1. You get a notification 🔔 and usually also a WhatsApp message from the customer.
2. Open the order. Tap **✅ Confirm order** – WhatsApp opens with a ready message. Fill in the delivery fee (`____ UGX`) and send.
3. If you agreed a delivery fee, tap **add** next to *Delivery* on the order so the total is right (optional).
4. When the customer pays by Mobile Money, check your MoMo SMS and tap **Mark as paid**.
5. When the rider leaves: **Mark as "Out for delivery"** and send **🛵 Out for delivery** on WhatsApp.
6. When delivered: **Mark as "Delivered"**.
7. If an order is cancelled: **Change status manually → Cancelled**. The items go back into stock.

## Discount codes (Codes)
Create a code like `WELCOME10` (10% off) and post it on Instagram / TikTok. You can limit how many times it can be used and until when.
The 10% loyalty reward on every 5th order works automatically.

## Reviews
New reviews wait in **Reviews** until you tap **Publish**. Delete spam or rude reviews.

## Merchant code
When you get your MoMoPay merchant code, put it in **More → MoMoPay merchant code**. Customers will then see the code instead of your number.
